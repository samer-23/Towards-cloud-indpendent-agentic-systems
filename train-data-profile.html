Before you model

Five things the schema does not tell you

Each of these was measured in the same full scan, and each one changes how you split, join, or impute.

1

id is not a primary key

222 of 1,188 ids appear in two or more studies — id=1 alone spans AZT1D, CTR3, Flair and HUPA-UCM, and ids 540–596 collide between Loop and OhioT1DM. Group and split on (source_file, id) or you will leak the same label across folds while believing subjects are disjoint.

2

65,993 rows are dated in the future

Four subjects carry timestamps past today, running to 2027-07-14 15:30. Any recency feature, train/validation cut by date, or "days since enrollment" term inherits the error.

3

Wearable channels reach dozens of subjects, not 1,730

steps covers 0.61% of rows and 57 subjects; heartrate 0.68% and 49; skin_temp and galvanic_skin_response 0.14% and 17 each. workout_intensity has 219 non-null values in the entire file. Treat these as a small side-cohort, not as features of the corpus.

4

Ethnicity is free text with duplicate codings

29 distinct levels, including both Black/African American (1.6%) and Black/African-American (0.4%) as separate values, plus compound entries like White, Hispanic/Latino. Normalize before any stratification or fairness claim.

5

The sensor clips the distribution at both ends

100,568 readings sit below 40 mg/dL and 290,998 above 400 — the reporting floor and ceiling of the CGM hardware, not physiology. The histogram above is drawn over 20–400, so that upper pile-up sits outside the frame. Tails are censored, which breaks a Gaussian likelihood on raw values.
